# Code of conduct

We want PieceOff to be a respectful, harassment-free space for everyone who
contributes or participates.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and welcome newcomers.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it kindly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sexualized language or imagery directed at others, or other conduct that
  would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull
requests that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants. Report problems to the maintainers through a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/UnsupervisedMedia/PieceOffApp/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@UnsupervisedMedia](https://github.com/UnsupervisedMedia).
